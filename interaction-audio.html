Design logic

Three related roles

01 / System & confirmation

Clarity in the everyday.

Conventional oscillator waveforms in Surge provide the tonal foundation for power, positive feedback, and pairing. Short gestures keep routine feedback compact; the pairing cue uses a C major seventh arpeggio.

02 / Warning continuum

Escalation through gesture.

Warnings I–III share a synthesis family created on the Korg MS-20 Mini. Timing and gesture become more urgent while retaining a related analog character.

03 / Critical state

A change in character.

The critical cue returns to Surge with wavetable synthesis. Its final three-hit burst shifts timbral character, marking a different category of system state.

Design constraint

Small speaker.
Clear distinctions.

The target is a hypothetical wearable speaker with constrained bandwidth. The design goal is to carry essential information within a compact spectral range, with severity expressed through gesture, timing, and density rather than large loudness jumps.

This is an independent audio concept study. Hardware playback and user recognition testing remain next steps.
