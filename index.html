Original Composition | Cinematic Scoring Study

Shutting You Out

An original instrumental composition exploring psychological tension, evolving electronic textures, unconventional instrumentation, and the interplay between musical structure and sonic space. Drawing on influences ranging from Vangelis to Daniel Lopatin and John Medeski, the composition uses timbre, dynamics, and spatial relationships to develop tension without relying on conventional cinematic gestures.

Composition, sound design, synthesis, arrangement, mixing, and production: Christopher Schor (Elvin T)
