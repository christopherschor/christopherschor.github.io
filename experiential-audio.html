The setting

A storm that
holds the thread.

I created this soundtrack for an escape room I made for friends, using music, environmental textures, and diegetic sound effects to heighten the narrative’s tension.

Rain and thunder sometimes establish the environment directly; elsewhere, they become compositional material. Heavily processed thunderclaps approach pink noise or take on the pops and clicks of dust on a record.

Between songs, connecting passages carry traces of what came before while preparing what follows. The storm persists through these transitions, shifting between the room’s imagined world and the musical structure of the soundtrack.
